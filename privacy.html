Privacy policy

hands-drive is used by one person only, its owner.

  • It asks Google only for the drive.file scope, so it can see only the files it created itself in the owner's Google Drive.
  • It collects no personal data, shares no data with anyone and sells nothing.
  • The authorization stays on the owner's server and can be revoked at any time from the Google account settings.
